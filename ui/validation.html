Sequence Model Test (pulse trains)
Tests a pulse-sequence model (trained on the Training page) on whole tracks, scored against the model's stored prototypes with its own thresholds. Fresh tracks are new tracks of the families the model was trained on — raise the missed / spurious pulse rates to test robustness to imperfect de-interleaving.
Testing on the dataset the model was trained on includes its training tracks, so accuracy will look better than on new data — prefer a different dataset or fresh tracks. Datasets are managed on the Dataset page; you can also upload a track file here (one row per pulse: track_id, pulse_idx, measurements, true_class_id), which is stored as a new dataset.
Try other thresholds on the trained model without retraining — e.g. a lower τ_d catches more unknown tracks but rejects more known windows. The verdict split and "unknown tracks flagged" change; accuracy and AUROC don't (they don't depend on thresholds).
